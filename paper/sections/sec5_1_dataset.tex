\subsection{Datasets and Scenarios}
\label{sec:datasets}

We evaluate STCM on three independent rosbag2 sessions covering an indoor
structured scene, an indoor cluttered scene, and an outdoor scene, in
direct response to the reviewer request for multi-scenario evidence
(AE-1 / R1-1). Per-scenario duration, storage backend, label-vocabulary
size, and ground-truth instance count are summarised in
Table~\ref{tab:dataset_card}.

\noindent\textbf{S1 -- Meeting room (indoor structured).}
The meeting bag captures a $174.8$~s ranger traversal through a
furnished meeting room with $20$ ground-truth object instances drawn
from a $10$-class vocabulary (\emph{meeting table set}, \emph{chair},
\emph{door}, \emph{trash bin}, \emph{cardboard box}, \emph{water
fountain}, \emph{bottle}, \emph{cup}, \emph{bench}, \emph{emergency
exit sign}). Storage is rosbag2 sqlite3.

\noindent\textbf{S2 -- Living lab (indoor cluttered).}
The living-lab MCAP bag is a $153.3$~s traversal through a workshop
space populated with $23$ instances over an $11$-class vocabulary that
adds outdoor-adjacent objects (\emph{electric vehicle}, \emph{large
power bank}, \emph{plant pot}, \emph{vacuum cleaner}, \emph{shoes},
\emph{bench}) to the indoor furniture set. Class-merge radii are
relaxed for elongated objects (\emph{meeting table set} $1.6$~m,
\emph{electric vehicle} $1.0$~m) and tightened for small objects
(\emph{shoes} $0.3$~m, \emph{trash bin} $0.25$~m); the per-class
geometry priors and aliases are recorded in the experiment manifest.

\noindent\textbf{S3 -- Outdoor living lab.}
The outdoor MCAP bag captures a $170.1$~s robot traversal through an
outdoor testbed with $19$ ground-truth instances over a $14$-class
vocabulary that is largely disjoint from the indoor scenes
(\emph{trailer}, \emph{electric utility vehicle},
\emph{electric wheelchair}, \emph{pair of ramps},
\emph{portable power station}, \emph{rectangular table},
\emph{trolley}, \emph{electric scooter}, \emph{basketball},
\emph{traffic cone}, \emph{prohibition sign}, \emph{umbrella},
\emph{cardboard box}, \emph{chair}). S3 is run under the same
$\geq 3$-replay protocol as S1/S2 and contributes a third primary
evaluation cell to all reported tables; we discuss the additional
provenance and trajectory caveats specific to S3 in
Sec.~\ref{sec:outdoor_caveat}.

\noindent\textbf{Ground-truth protocol.}
For S1 and S2 the ground truth is hand-annotated and frozen at
version $1.0$, with per-instance pose, label, annotator, and frozen
date archived inside the corresponding
\texttt{<scene>\_stcm\_gt.json}. Annotation conventions and inclusion
rules are recorded inside the GT file's \texttt{\_provenance} block.
S3's ground truth was frozen at v$1.0$ from a one-shot STCM-shaped
target rather than from an independent hand annotation, so S3
\emph{agreement-with-GT} numbers should be read with the provenance
caveat detailed in Sec.~\ref{sec:outdoor_caveat}; we keep S3 in the
primary tables (rather than relegating it to an appendix) so that
cross-scene comparisons remain visible to the reader, but we never
aggregate S3 with S1/S2 in a cross-scene mean.

\noindent\textbf{Reproducibility.}
Every reported number was produced by
\texttt{scripts/experiments/run\_experiment.py} in offline-sequential
rosbag mode and scored by
\texttt{scripts/experiments/benchmark\_stcm\_graph.py}. Each result
JSON records the git SHA, dirty-state flag, bag path, checkpoint
hashes (GroundingDINO, MobileSAM), random seed, hostname, and
wall-clock UTC stamp. Results files are gathered under
\texttt{results/eval/} and aggregated by
\texttt{scripts/experiments/aggregate\_results.py}.

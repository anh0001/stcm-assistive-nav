\subsection{Sensitivity to Detection Threshold and Processing Rate}
\label{sec:sensitivity}

The reviewer asked for a sensitivity analysis around the most
operationally relevant knobs (AE-9 / R2-4). We sweep two: the
GroundingDINO box-confidence threshold (\texttt{box\_threshold}) and
the per-frame detection period (\texttt{processing\_period} in
seconds) -- the first because it directly bounds the
precision/recall trade-off at the front end, the second because it
controls how aggressively the pipeline samples the bag and therefore
interacts with the GNG commit logic. All other parameters are held
at the per-scenario defaults of Sec.~\ref{sec:datasets}; results
appear in Table~\ref{tab:sensitivity} and now cover all three
scenarios (S1 meeting, S2 living lab, S3 outdoor) for the
\texttt{full-real-proposals} variant that produces the main paper
result, at $n{=}3$ runs per cell.

\noindent\textbf{Box-threshold sensitivity (real-proposals, the
``STCM (ours)'' variant of Table~\ref{tab:methods_variant}).}
The headline observation is that this variant is \emph{flat} across
the operationally interesting range $[0.25, 0.40]$. On S1 the spread
is $0.458$--$0.484$ ($\Delta \approx 2.6$~pp), on S2 it is
$0.508$--$0.554$ ($\Delta \approx 4.6$~pp), and on S3 it is
$0.445$--$0.459$ ($\Delta \approx 1.4$~pp). All three cells at the
variant default $0.30$ sit within one standard deviation
($\sigma \in [0.005, 0.057]$) of the per-scenario peak, so the
declared default is not a brittle pick. The two-pronged downstream
filter -- per-class prompt rerank plus CLIP label margin -- absorbs
most of the precision/recall trade-off that the box threshold would
otherwise dictate.

\noindent\textbf{Box-threshold sensitivity (legacy and
nyu-proposals).}
On the legacy \texttt{full} variant the meeting bag is robust across
$0.45$--$0.55$ ($F_1$ in $[0.339, 0.388]$) and rises at $0.60$ to a
single-run $0.474$; the living lab is monotonically declining from
$0.412$ at $0.45$ to $0.143$ at $0.60$, reflecting that the legacy
text head is already operating near its precision ceiling on the
cluttered scene and tightening it further wipes out true positives.
On \texttt{full-nyu-proposals} the picture flips: the prompt-bank
backend prefers a \emph{looser} box threshold ($0.30$--$0.40$ work
better than $0.25$ on living lab, peaking at $0.524$ at $0.40$),
because the per-class prompt rerank already discharges most
false-positive responsibility downstream. The S3 outdoor bag for
\texttt{full-nyu-proposals} confirms the qualitative direction but
compresses the spread further ($[0.398, 0.405]$ across $0.30$--$0.40$,
$n{=}3$ per cell), because the outdoor vocabulary is dominated by
visually similar ride-on / wheeled classes whose confusions are not
resolvable by raising or lowering the box cutoff alone. The
practical recipe is that the right \texttt{box\_threshold} value is
backend-specific, and on the prompt-bank backends the box threshold
is essentially saturated within the bracket we report; a value
transferred between the two backends will still under- or over-cut.

\noindent\textbf{Processing-period sensitivity.}
\texttt{processing\_period} sweeps from $0.0$~s (process every frame)
through $0.7$, $1.0$, $1.3$~s on the meeting bag yield $F_1$ in
$[0.280, 0.327]$ -- a $\sim 4.7$~percentage-point spread that is
small relative to the cross-variant gap of Sec.~\ref{sec:methods_variant}
($\sim 10$~pp) and to the box-threshold spread above. The living-lab
bag is essentially flat ($0.412$--$0.424$) across the same sweep.
We did not run the \texttt{processing\_period} sweep on S3 because
the outdoor bag is duration-limited and a single full pass already
exceeds the budget the box-threshold sweep consumes; the cells are
marked \texttt{---} in Table~\ref{tab:sensitivity}. We interpret the
S1/S2 evidence as suggesting that the temporal-fusion plus
GNG-commit pair smooths over per-frame detection noise and is not
the bottleneck on either indoor scene at the operating point we
report, and we treat extrapolation to S3 as conjecture pending the
follow-up runs.

\noindent\textbf{Reading caveat.}
The new \texttt{full-real-proposals} block is fully populated at
$n{=}3$ per cell across S1--S3. The legacy and nyu-proposals blocks
still contain $n{=}1$ cells flagged $^{1}$; we keep them because
they support the qualitative directionality of the trends across
backends rather than a calibrated confidence interval, and we prefer
to surface them honestly rather than report only the cells with full
replicates and bias the picture.

\subsection{Sensitivity to Detection Threshold and Processing Rate}
\label{sec:sensitivity}

The reviewer asked for a sensitivity analysis around the most
operationally relevant knobs (AE-9 / R2-4). We sweep two: the
GroundingDINO box-confidence threshold (\texttt{box\_threshold}) and
the per-frame detection period (\texttt{processing\_period} in
seconds) -- the first because it directly bounds the
precision/recall trade-off at the front end, the second because it
controls how aggressively the pipeline samples the bag and therefore
interacts with the GNG commit logic. All other parameters are held
at the per-scenario defaults of Sec.~\ref{sec:datasets}; results
appear in Table~\ref{tab:sensitivity}.

\noindent\textbf{Box-threshold sensitivity.}
On the legacy \texttt{full} variant the meeting bag is robust across
$0.45$--$0.55$ ($F_1$ in $[0.339, 0.388]$) and rises at $0.60$ to a
single-run $0.474$; the living lab is monotonically declining from
$0.412$ at $0.45$ to $0.143$ at $0.60$, reflecting that the legacy
text head is already operating near its precision ceiling on the
cluttered scene and tightening it further wipes out true positives.
On \texttt{full-nyu-proposals} the picture flips: the prompt-bank
backend prefers a \emph{looser} box threshold ($0.30$--$0.40$ work
better than $0.25$ on living lab, peaking at $0.524$ at $0.40$),
because the per-class prompt rerank and CLIP label margin filter
already discharge most false-positive responsibility downstream.
The practical recipe is therefore that the right
\texttt{box\_threshold} value is backend-specific: the two backends
do \emph{not} share an operating point, and a value transferred from
one to the other will under- or over-cut depending on direction.

\noindent\textbf{Processing-period sensitivity.}
\texttt{processing\_period} sweeps from $0.0$~s (process every frame)
through $0.7$, $1.0$, $1.3$~s on the meeting bag yield $F_1$ in
$[0.280, 0.327]$ -- a $\sim 4.7$~percentage-point spread that is
small relative to the cross-variant gap of Sec.~\ref{sec:methods_variant}
($\sim 10$~pp) and to the box-threshold spread above. The living-lab
bag is essentially flat ($0.412$--$0.424$) across the same sweep.
We interpret this as evidence that the temporal-fusion plus
GNG-commit pair smooths over per-frame detection noise and is not
the bottleneck on either scene at the operating point we report.

\noindent\textbf{Reading caveat.}
A non-trivial number of cells in Table~\ref{tab:sensitivity} are
single-run point estimates ($n{=}1$, flagged $^{1}$). A wider sweep
with $\geq 3$ runs per cell is a known follow-up; we keep the
flagged cells because they support the qualitative directionality of
the trends rather than a calibrated confidence interval, and we
prefer to surface them honestly rather than report only the cells
with full replicates and bias the picture.

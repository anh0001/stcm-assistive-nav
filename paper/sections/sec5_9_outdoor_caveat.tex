\subsection{Outdoor Scene: Caveats and Limitations}
\label{sec:outdoor_caveat}

S3 is reported as a third primary evaluation cell in
Tables~\ref{tab:dataset_card}--\ref{tab:grounding}. The current
S3 best-replicated cell is $F_1{=}0.456 \pm 0.005$ under
\texttt{full-real-proposals} v16 ($n{=}3$,
\texttt{max\_observation\_range\_m}~$=5.0$,
\texttt{cross\_label\_merge\_distance\_m}~$=0.8$, per-class
\texttt{box\_threshold}~$=0.35$ on five FP-heavy classes:
\textit{prohibition sign}, \textit{rectangular table},
\textit{electric wheelchair}, \textit{portable power station},
\textit{pair of ramps}), reported under the outdoor per-class
radius and alias scoring policy declared in the manifest. This
beats the prior-augmented stack \texttt{full-nyu-proposals + ESANet}
($F_1{=}0.419 \pm 0.004$, $n{=}3$) by $+0.037$, Welch
$t{\approx}10.0$, $p{<}10^{-4}$. The earlier \texttt{full-nyu-proposals} S3 cell --
$F_1{=}0.414 \pm 0.014$ at git SHA \texttt{856c756}
(\texttt{box\_threshold}~$=0.35$,
\texttt{max\_observation\_range\_m}~$=3.0$) -- is retained as a
historical reference for the perception-backend regression test;
the navigation, semantic, and grounding tables (B, C, E) have
been re-rendered under \texttt{full-real-proposals} (v7 hybrid on
S1, v9 on S2, v16 on S3, $n{=}3$ per cell), which lifts S2
macro-$F_1$ by $+0.279$ ($0.298 \rightarrow 0.577$), S3
macro-$F_1$ by $+0.121$ ($0.288 \rightarrow 0.409$), S2
grounding overall top-1 by $+17.8$ pp, and S3 grounding overall
top-1 by $+11.1$ pp. Under strict same-label scoring
at a global $1.0$~m match radius (the indoor S1/S2 contract), the
historical \texttt{full-nyu-proposals} three-replay set scores
$F_1{=}0.264 \pm 0.021$. We report the per-class/alias number as
the S3 headline because it matches the outdoor-scale geometry of
the bag (e.g.\ trailers at $3$--$5$~m extent), but we report both
numbers explicitly so the reader can read S3 either under the
outdoor-scale contract (against which the perception is tuned)
or under the strict indoor contract (against which S1 and S2 are
tuned). On 2026-05-09 we raised
\texttt{cross\_label\_merge\_distance\_m} from $0.45$ to $0.8$~m
as the outdoor manifest default after an autonomous
review-and-fix loop (Codex GPT-5.5 as external reviewer) flagged
GNG over-commit as the dominant FP source on S3; the same-scorer
lift attributable to that knob alone is $+0.033$ absolute
($0.381 \pm 0.006 \rightarrow 0.414 \pm 0.014$ under
per-class/alias scoring), with TP$=12$ stable across all runs and
FP dropping from $\sim 27$ to $\sim 25$ per run. This section
discusses the remaining caveats specific to S3 that do not apply
to S1 or S2.

\noindent\textbf{Caveat 1 -- ground-truth provenance.}
S3 was hand-annotated from rosbag2 RGB and LiDAR replay following
the same protocol as S1 and S2, but its GT JSON was \emph{late-
stamped}: unlike S1/S2, the frozen S3 file initially lacked a
\texttt{\_provenance} block and still retained STCM runtime
metadata from the output used as the annotation draft. The
2026-05-07 internal audit (\texttt{EXPERIMENT\_AUDIT.md}) failed S3
on structural provenance, not on evidence that annotation did not
occur. The \texttt{\_provenance} block has since been stamped
(annotator, frozen date, inclusion protocol, source bag, and the
note that the STCM output was manually corrected into GT) and the
runtime telemetry stripped. S3 scores should therefore be read
with the documentation-gap caveat described here, not as a proxy-
GT result. We do not claim S3 is independently double-annotated
ground truth, only that it is annotator-curated GT under the same
protocol as S1/S2.

\noindent\textbf{Caveat 2 -- aliases and scoring conservativeness.}
S3 has two scoring regimes that are reported side-by-side:

(i)~\emph{outdoor per-class / alias} scoring (the headline
$F_1{=}0.414 \pm 0.014$): per-class match radii from $1.5$~m
(default) to $4.0$~m (\emph{trailer}) declared in
\texttt{configs/experiments/manifest.yaml}, plus an outdoor alias
set that admits e.g.\ \emph{trolley $\to$ electric utility
vehicle}. This regime matches the outdoor-scale geometry of the
bag and is the \emph{primary} reporting contract.

(ii)~\emph{strict $1.0$~m, no aliases} scoring (the indoor S1/S2
contract): $F_1{=}0.264 \pm 0.021$ on the same three runs.

The gap between the two regimes is $\sim 0.15$ absolute. The
larger fraction of that gap comes from the per-class match radii
(outdoor objects are large; a $1.0$~m centroid radius is
inappropriate for $3$--$5$~m trailers); a smaller fraction comes
from the alias set, which is small and was set deliberately
because the outdoor prompt bank does not include the
GT-canonical labels for two classes. We flag the
\emph{rectangular table $\rightarrow$ trailer} alias as
semantically suspect; dropping it would lower the per-class
$F_1$ by $\sim 0.02$. A future S3 evaluation that uses the
indoor contract should cite the strict $0.264 \pm 0.021$
number; the rest of this paper reports the per-class
$0.414 \pm 0.014$ figure as the S3 headline.

\noindent\textbf{Caveat 3 -- trajectory-bound recall.}
A failure-mode analysis on the replicated S3 cell finds that $4$
of the $11$ false negatives are trajectory-bound: three GT
trailers in the south-west quadrant and one prohibition sign sit
outside the bag's trajectory bounding box, so the robot never
observes them, and a range gate of $3$~m -- the manifest default
that minimises horizon false positives -- mechanically excludes
them. Loosening the range gate to $10$~m to recover the lost
trailers floods the scene with horizon FPs and \emph{drops} $F_1$
on the same bag. The remaining $7$ FN are detector-rerank issues
(e.g.\ the NYU CLIP rerank never selects \emph{umbrella}). We
call this out because it bounds achievable recall on this specific
bag regardless of perception-backend choice and is not addressable
by knob-tuning alone.

\noindent\textbf{Caveat 4 -- run-to-run variance reflects
threshold churn, not seed variance.}
The reported S3 standard deviations ($\pm 0.001$ on macro-$F_1$,
$\pm 3.8\%$ on nav success) come from sweeping
\texttt{box\_threshold} across the three runs rather than from
varying a random seed under a fixed configuration. STCM's offline-
sequential bag replay is deterministic at fixed-config + fixed-bag,
so what we report on Tables~\ref{tab:semantic}--\ref{tab:grounding}
is \emph{replay repeatability under threshold perturbation}, not
seed-level stochastic replication. Seed-perturbed replicates are
queued as follow-up; this also applies to the S1 and S2 cells with
$\sigma{=}0.0\%$ entries (Sec.~\ref{sec:navigation},
\ref{sec:semantic}, \ref{sec:grounding}), which are
deterministic-replay agreement, not independent replication.

\noindent\textbf{Caveat 5 -- a separate DSE point is archived but
not reported as a paper number.}
A $27$-iteration design-space exploration of the perception-
backend knobs and the per-class prompt bank, archived in
\texttt{dse\_results/DSE\_REPORT.md}, identified an unreplicated
single-seed configuration with a higher score on the proxy GT.
We do \emph{not} promote that point to a paper number because
(i)~it is single-seed and selected on the same benchmark it is
scored on, (ii)~the eval-protocol contract of $\geq 3$ runs
(Sec.~\ref{sec:datasets}) is not met for that configuration, and
(iii)~Caveats 1 and 2 above apply to that configuration with the
same force. The DSE point exists only as an internal record of
which knobs the harness explored; it is not a published result.

\noindent\textbf{What the S3 number does and does not show.}
The replicated S3 cell \emph{does} show that the indoor-tuned
NYU-grounded backend, paired with an outdoor-specific prompt bank
and a $3$~m range gate, produces a non-trivial graph for an
out-of-distribution outdoor scene with reproducible run-to-run
agreement under threshold perturbation. The cell \emph{does not}
show that STCM achieves a particular $F_1$ outdoors in general,
that the headline number is comparable to S1/S2 cells without the
proxy-GT caveat, or that the value would survive an independent
hand-annotated S3 GT. Reviewers asked for outdoor evidence
(AE-1 / R1-1); we report a single headline number under explicit
proxy-GT scope rather than a cherry-picked alternative.
